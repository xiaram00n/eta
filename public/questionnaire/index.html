Better-arranged rides start with your real work.

Taxi operators asked, we listened.

ETA is a proposed booking and trip-coordination service for St. Thomas transportation businesses. Guests would request rides; participating operators would decide whether to accept the booking request, and ETA would handle the rest. We would coordinate the driver, vehicle and pickup; manage changes; and provide updates to the guest on the operator's behalf. This would offer guests a more efficient, convenient and faster way to travel, while helping operators receive consistent bookings without the hassle of coordinating each booking.

The guest prototype shows the traveler’s side. The operator tools are still a proposal. Your answers help us decide whether those tools solve a real problem, what they must do and what they must preserve.

Driving guests and showing them around the island is what you do best. Understanding the work and designing systems that support it is what we do best.

VI Transportation Operator Questionnaire

Eight short sections about bookings, tools you use, work you complete, money you receive and spend, and time spent coordinating. Your answers guide what to build; they do not assume a solution.

Every question is optional. Estimates and “not sure” are useful. No documents, customer lists, account details or other sensitive information are needed.

Answers stay in this browser on this device until you clear the draft or a submission is confirmed. On a shared device, clear the draft when you finish. You will review your answers before choosing to submit them to ETA.
