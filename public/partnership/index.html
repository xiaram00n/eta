Working on this together

I’m developing software for existing taxi, limo and tour businesses in St. Thomas. Before deciding what to build, I need to understand how operators work and what they would actually use.

I’d like your help with that part.

What I’m proposing

You bring your experience as a driver and operator, help me ask the right questions, and introduce me to people willing to share how their businesses work.

I handle the product design and technical work, organize what we learn, and build examples we can test together.

There is no upfront cash contribution from you in this discovery arrangement. Let’s agree on the time involved and any expenses before starting.

What stays with the operator

Their business name, customer relationships, vehicles and authority over pricing. The software should fit how they run their business—not require them to hand it over.

We are not proposing a new transportation carrier or asking you to run a booking desk for us.

What we need to learn

  • How requests become confirmed, completed and paid bookings.
  • Where time or money is lost, and what already works well.
  • What guests ask for and where arrangements break down.
  • How drivers share work, use radio or messaging, and respect existing relationships.
  • Which requirements need checking with the relevant authorities.
  • Whether operators would pay for a specific, tested improvement.

Introductions should be voluntary. We do not need guest names, private customer lists, banking details or copies of licenses.

A small first step

  1. Walk through a recent booking together.
  2. Invite a small, varied group of operators to answer the questionnaire.
  3. Review the results and choose one problem to test.
  4. Try a prototype with willing operators, then decide whether to continue.

We should agree on your role, time, expenses and how we use information before asking you to do this work.

What is not agreed yet

Ownership, compensation, revenue sharing, decision-making and any longer-term responsibilities are still open. This walkthrough does not settle those terms or promise an equity share.

We need to agree and document those terms before either of us takes on an ongoing role.
